POCKET MYTH marks National Day with a week of rewards

The National Day event runs October 1–7 with sign-in rewards, a prayer-coin draw, map treasure chests, a jigsaw puzzle for Diancie and the Gym Trail event.

As autumn arrives, POCKET MYTH invites trainers to a week-long celebration packed with rewards and battles.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact rewards and rules follow the in-game schedule.

Sign-in rewards (October 1 – October 7)

Log in to claim a stack of rewards:

  • Ultra-rare Pokémon random pack
  • UR TM gift pack (random)
  • SSR TM selection pack
  • SSR held-item selection pack
  • Master Ball
  • Golden Perfume
  • Lv3 Charm selection pack

Stamina, Crystals, Gacha Tickets and other daily resources are included as well.

Prayer-coin draw (October 1 – October 7)

Top up during the event to receive Prayer Coins, then spend them on draws. The pool contains Poké Balls of several qualities, each with its own reward tier. Cumulative draws unlock Shiny Miracle Star Jirachi, high-grade material selection packs, Golden Crowns and other rare items.

Map treasure chests (October 1 – October 7)

Limited-time chests refresh on the map. Each day you can visit Gen 1 towns to collect Bronze, Silver and Golden chests, each with a different reward table. Golden chests can drop treasure-hunt puzzle pieces, Tera Shards and hunting entry tickets. Cumulative collections also pay out starter shard selection packs.

Jigsaw puzzle (October 1 – October 7)

Complete the daily Pokémon tasks to earn Pokémon shards, and collect chests for a chance at puzzle pieces. Finish the whole picture to unlock the event's limited Pokémon, Diancie.

Gym Trail returns (October 1 – October 7)

The popular Gym Trail event is back. Join with a Trail entry ticket and push the map forward as a server; activating tiles and completing battle or dispatch nodes earns server-wide loot and personal rewards, while skills and blessings help you explore further.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
