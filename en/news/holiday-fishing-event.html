POCKET MYTH holiday fishing event adds four reward shops

Fishing, a four-shop catch shop, album rewards, a top-up SP Pokémon, the Caterpie evolution event and Wolf Is Coming — August 7-13.

The start of autumn and the holidays combine for one last taste of summer. POCKET MYTH's "Holiday Arrival" event opens soon with several modes running together.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Holiday Arrival · fishing sign-in (August 7 – August 13)

A seven-day sign-in runs during the event. Daily rewards include Summer Bait, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Holiday Arrival · fishing time (August 7 – August 13)

Cast your line and let skill and luck do the rest. The fishing mode pays out Summer Fish Coins and Summer Koi Passes. As your total catch weight grows you unlock four shops — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Fish King — each stocking upgrade materials and rare items.

The Fish King shop accepts the Summer Koi Pass for top-tier rewards including a Shiny SP Pokémon selection pack, Immortal Sword & Shield shards and legendary UR Pokémon stones.

Holiday Arrival · legend album (August 7 – August 13)

Complete the album to claim Shiny Garchomp and its Garchompite Mega Stone.

Holiday Arrival · top-up reward (August 7 – August 13)

Reach the cumulative top-up target during the event to receive the new limited SP Pokémon Shiny Abyss Sword & Shield, plus its exclusive TM King's Sword & Shield.

Holiday Arrival · battle playground (August 7 – August 13)

A flip-and-match challenge; clear the stages to claim bait items, universal card packs and other rewards at no cost.

Holiday Arrival · Rayquaza evolution (August 10 – August 13)

The Caterpie evolution event opens for a limited time. Once 100 gift packs have been purchased across the server the evolution path unlocks, and the first 800 trainers to reach the goal can evolve Caterpie into Rayquaza.

Holiday Arrival · Wolf Is Coming (August 7 – August 13)

The "Wolf Is Coming" mode returns. Spend Wolf Coins to choose your grassland and dodge the wolf's attack; a successful dodge splits the coins of everyone who failed and builds points, which can be exchanged for rewards.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
