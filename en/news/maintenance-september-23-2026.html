SP Ghost King Gengar joins POCKET MYTH on September 23

Maintenance on September 23 adds SP Ghost King Gengar, Season 11 content, daily-task quality-of-life changes and three battle fixes.

POCKET MYTH will go offline for maintenance on September 23, 2026 from 08:00 to 10:00, expected to take about 2 hours. If the work overruns, the servers will reopen later.

New content

A new SP-rarity ultra beast joins the game: Ghost King Gengar (Poison / Ghost).

Ability — Ghost Body: takes no damage other than from attacks; removes its Poison and Ghost weaknesses; when it faints, it takes the opponent down with it; keeps itself in a levitating state.

Season 11 content is added at the same time.

Quality-of-life changes

  • Reduced daily busywork: the "daily must-do" list is hidden and its rewards move to Daily Tasks
  • Monthly card holders can complete Daily Tasks with one tap

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Photon Geyser not ignoring the Sturdy ability
  • Fixed the Ice Lord ability triggering the opponent's Defiant twice
  • Fixed a Red Card held item still triggering after the holder was knocked out

Maintenance compensation

600 Crystals + 10 Gacha Tickets + 2 Shiny Coins.

Thanks for your understanding — log in after the update to claim the pack.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
