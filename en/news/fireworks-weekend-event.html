Brilliant Fireworks weekend lands in POCKET MYTH

A five-day fireworks weekend runs October 23–27 with sign-in rewards, a server-wide fireworks celebration, a dice-board mode and a merge mini-game.

POCKET MYTH's "Brilliant Fireworks" event opens soon, with several modes running together and rare Pokémon and items on the line.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Five-day sign-in (October 23 – October 27)

A five-day sign-in runs during the event. Daily rewards include Brilliant Fireworks, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Fireworks celebration (October 23 – October 27)

Take part in the fireworks mode to trigger server-wide red packets, share the festival luck and collect upgrade resources and rare items.

This round's fireworks rewards include Gold, Crystals and Mystery Capsules, plus a chance at SP Dark Ho-Oh, a Silver Crown selection pack, a Nature Mint gift pack and an Lv5 Charm pack.

Fireworks vouchers (October 23 – October 27)

Collect the exclusive item Brilliant Voucher during the event and exchange it in the shop for high-end upgrade materials. The shop is refreshed this round with an ultra-beast evolution stone selection box, Dark Phoenix exclusive held items and a Dawn/Dusk held-item selection box.

Dice board (October 23 – October 27)

The classic roll-and-move board returns. Clear stages to bank event resources and a long list of rewards.

Merge mini-game (October 23 – October 26)

A relaxing merge game: combine Poké Balls to build points and trade them for practical upgrade materials, including Silver Crown shard selections and UR/SSR universal held-item shards.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
