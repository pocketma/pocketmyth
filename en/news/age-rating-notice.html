POCKET MYTH age rating and player protection notice

POCKET MYTH is rated 16+. This notice covers the rating, real-name verification and the limits that apply on the Simplified Chinese servers.

Dear trainers,

This notice describes the age rating for POCKET MYTH and the player-protection rules that apply on the Simplified Chinese servers.

Age rating

POCKET MYTH is a pocket-style card game suitable for users aged 16 and over. Minors are encouraged to play under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

What the game is

POCKET MYTH is a high-definition 3D pocket-style mobile game set in a pocket world. Players take the role of a trainer, collect and raise Pokémon, and take part in a wide range of battles on the way to becoming the strongest trainer.

Real-name verification

The game includes a real-name verification system. Accounts verified as belonging to a minor are subject to the limits below, and some items require payment.

  • Users under 8: cannot make purchases
  • Users aged 8 to 15: no more than CNY 50 per transaction and CNY 200 per month
  • Users aged 16 to 17: no more than CNY 100 per transaction and CNY 400 per month

Play windows for minors

Verified minor accounts can log in on Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays and statutory holidays between 20:00 and 21:00 only; login is unavailable outside those windows.

These rules are set by the Chinese publishing regulations and apply to the Simplified Chinese servers. Players on other regional servers should refer to the terms shown in their own client.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
