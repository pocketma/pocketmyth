POCKET MYTH Pokedex entry No.021 Spearow

Spearow in the POCKET MYTH Pokedex: a Normal and Flying bird of prey with Keen Eye and Sturdy, its ecology, stats and evolution.

Basic Data

  • Type: Normal + Flying
  • Category: Bird of Prey Pokémon
  • Height: 0.3m | Weight: 2.0kg
  • Ability: Keen Eye (accuracy cannot be lowered) / Sturdy (a fatal hit at full HP leaves it with 1 HP)
  • Hidden Ability: Sheer Force (when using a move with a side effect, the side effect disappears but power drops by 20%)

Form and Ecology

Spearow is an aggressive Pokémon found all over the Kanto region, blending the features of a sparrow and a small bird of prey: dark brown feathers cover its head and back, its belly is grey-white, it has a signature red mask-like marking around its eyes, and its beak is sharp and curved, suited to pecking insects and Berries. Its wings are short but strong; it cannot fly high for long, but it can dive-attack at explosive speed (about 60 km/h), and the black spots at the tips of its tail feathers are a flock identification mark.

A highly aggressive social creature, Spearow usually holds treetops or cliffs in groups of 5 to 10 and is fiercely territorial. Wild Spearow will attack creatures that enter their territory on their own initiative, even chasing Pokémon ten times their size. Research shows their aggression is linked to how fast their beak grows: an adult Spearow's beak grows 2cm per month and has to be worn down by pecking hard objects such as rocks and metal, which explains why they so often attack human belongings.

Ability Analysis

Base stats: 262 total, with Attack (60) and Speed (71) as its core strengths and Sp. Def (40) and Defense (35) as its weaker points, a classic fast offensive pressure role.

Ability applications:

  • Keen Eye: keeps accuracy intact against moves such as White Mist and evasion boosts, making it good against evasion strategies such as Gengar
  • Sturdy: a rescue ability that can tank one fatal hit at full HP (such as Seismic Toss) and buy a switch for a teammate
  • Sheer Force builds: paired with moves such as Take Down and Hyper Beam, trading 20% power for no recoil, good for fast doubles play

Evolution Line

Level 20 -> Fearow (Normal + Flying)

After evolving it grows to 0.8m and 18.0kg, its beak lengthens to 15cm, the feathers on its back turn grey-black, and its tail feathers spread into a fan. The Alola region has a special form (Dark + Flying type) with deep purple feathers and a hook-curved beak, adapted to the ecology of city landfills.
