POCKET MYTH maintenance on September 10 fixes three issues

Maintenance on September 10 from 08:00 to 10:00 fixes Water Absorb, battle chat toggles and Moonlight healing, with compensation.

POCKET MYTH will go offline for maintenance on September 10, 2026 from 08:00 to 10:00, expected to take about 2 hours. If the work overruns, the servers will reopen later — the in-game notice is the final word.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Pokémon with the Water Absorb ability taking Water-type damage from a Pokémon behind Substitute
  • Fixed the battle assistant toggle being bound to the chat toggle
  • Fixed Moonlight's healing amount not being affected by the weather

Maintenance compensation

600 Crystals + 10 Gacha Tickets + 2 Shiny Coins.

Sorry for the interruption — log in after the update to collect the pack.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
