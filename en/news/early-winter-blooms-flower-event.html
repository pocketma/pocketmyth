Flowers return to POCKET MYTH for an early winter event

The classic Flowers event returns November 6-9 with sign-in rewards, flower tributes, a custom Starlight Treasure and a Shiny Lucario album reward.

POCKET MYTH's classic "Flowers" event opens soon, with several systems running at the same time. Rare Pokémon and a long list of items are up for grabs, so join the trainers for a late-autumn run packed with rewards.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Four-day sign-in (November 6 – November 9)

Log in during the event to claim progression help and event items. Daily rewards include Flowers, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets, so every mode stays within reach.

Flower tribute (November 6 – November 9)

Collect Flowers and offer them as tribute to raise your Heart level. Each level pays out tier rewards and Flower Vouchers, and the Vouchers can be traded in the exchange shop for rare items and upgrade materials. The grand prize this time is SP Sword & Shield — offer a romantic tribute and take the powerful Pokémon home.

Offering Flowers also grants the event item Flower Voucher, which unlocks a large selection of high-end upgrade items in the shop.

Starlight Treasure (November 6 – November 9)

Open the exclusive Starlight Treasure and choose the rewards you actually want. The pool is customisable, all prizes are guaranteed within 12 rounds, and each draw has a chance to trigger a bonus round.

Flip-card matching (November 6 – November 9)

Clear the matching mini-game stage by stage. Difficulty rises as you go, but each cleared stage pays out card-pack shards and Flowers.

Album collection

Complete the event album to claim Shiny Lucario and its Mega Stone.

Caterpie evolution event

The Caterpie evolution event runs for a limited time. Once 100 gift packs have been purchased across the server, the Caterpie evolution path unlocks; the first 800 trainers on the server to reach the evolution goal can evolve Caterpie into Rayquaza.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
