POCKET MYTH Elite Cup report for August

2,895 trainers entered the August Elite Cup: Water was the most played type, Fairy posted the highest score.

Hello trainers! The latest Elite Cup data is in. Thanks to the community member Tianya for compiling it — let's look at how the meta shifted this month.

The figures below cover the official servers only. Feel free to add your own observations in the comments.

August at a glance

  • Total participants: 2,895
  • Most played: Water (451), Flying (421), Ground (366)
  • Highest score (1st place): Fairy (3,750), Ghost (3,550), Dark (3,150)
  • Highest top-100 cutoff: Water (1,450 points), Ghost (1,350 points)

Water keeps the largest player base and the toughest cutoff, while Fairy and Ghost continue to convert their popularity into top finishes. Dark remains efficient: fewer players, but a high ceiling.
