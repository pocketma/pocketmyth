POCKET MYTH maintenance on August 27 adds backpacks

Maintenance on August 27 adds protagonist backpacks from five regions and fixes two scoring and ability-shield issues.

POCKET MYTH will go offline for maintenance on August 27, 2026 from 08:00 to 11:00, expected to take about 3 hours. If the work overruns, the servers will reopen later.

New content

New character backpacks: the Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire protagonists (Brendan and May), the Sword & Shield protagonists (Victor and Gloria), the Scarlet & Violet protagonists (Florian and Juliana), the Black & White protagonists (Hilbert and Hilda), and the Sun & Moon protagonists (Elio and Selene).

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Pokémon knocked out by weather effects not scoring correctly
  • Fixed Photon Geyser's ability-ignoring effect not triggering the Ability Shield held item

Maintenance compensation

600 Crystals + 10 Gacha Tickets + 2 Shiny Coins.

Thanks for bearing with us — log in after the update to claim the pack.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
