Golden Autumn weekend brings SP Lunala to POCKET MYTH

The Golden Autumn weekend runs October 17–20 with sign-in rewards, a flower contract for SP Lunala, a flip-card mode, an album reward and Starlight Treasure.

POCKET MYTH's "Golden Autumn" event opens soon, with multiple modes running at once and rare Pokémon and items to collect.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Daily sign-in (October 17 – October 20)

A four-day sign-in runs during the event. Daily rewards include Golden Autumn Fireworks, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Flower contract (October 17 – October 20)

Collect Flowers and offer them as tribute to raise your Heart level. Level rewards include Flower Vouchers, which the exchange shop accepts for rare items and upgrade materials. The headline prize is SP Lunala.

Battle playground (October 17 – October 20)

A flip-and-match challenge: clear the stages to claim Flowers, universal card packs and more at no cost.

Flip-card carnival (October 17 – October 20)

The flip-card mini-game returns for a limited time. Clear the final stage to earn Zygarde — a good chance for anyone who missed the previous run.

Legend album (October 17 – October 20)

Complete the album to claim Shiny Shaymin and a large number of shards.

Starlight Treasure (October 17 – October 20)

Open the exclusive Starlight Treasure and choose your own rewards. The pool is customisable, every prize is guaranteed within 12 rounds, and bonus rounds can trigger during the draw.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
