Halloween comes to POCKET MYTH with pumpkins and SP Gengar

Halloween runs October 29 to November 1 with pumpkin sign-in, a pumpkin tribute for SP Gengar, flip-card matching and a lucky-colour Eevee table.

Halloween in POCKET MYTH runs from October 29 to November 1, with a pumpkin-themed sign-in, a tribute system, a flip-card mini-game and a lucky-colour Eevee table.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Halloween sign-in (October 29 – November 1)

A four-day sign-in runs during the event. Log in to claim progression help and event items; daily rewards include Halloween Pumpkins, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Halloween tribute (October 29 – November 1)

Don't hoard the pumpkins you collect — offer them as tribute to earn Pumpkin Cards and a few surprises. Collect enough cards and the headline prize, SP Gengar, is yours.

Offering pumpkins also grants the exclusive item Halloween Pumpkin Card, which can be exchanged for a large set of high-end upgrade items.

Flip-card matching (October 29 – November 1)

Take part in the Halloween matching mini-game. Each stage is a little harder than the last; match the same patterns to clear stages and earn Halloween Pumpkins.

Complete the album collection as well to claim a random or self-selected Shiny ultra-rare Pokémon pack.

Pick your lucky Eevee (October 30 – November 1)

Choose the Eevee colour you want, buy card packs with Crystals and collect Eevees.

Scoring

  • Lucky colour drawn: +1
  • Three in a row: +2
  • Clear the table: +3
  • Full family photo: +6

Exchange rewards

  • Standard table: each Eevee settles into 1 starter shard selection pack
  • Advanced table: each Eevee settles into 1 high-grade material selection pack

Different ratings pay extra rewards on top, so luck decides who takes home the Eevee crown today.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
