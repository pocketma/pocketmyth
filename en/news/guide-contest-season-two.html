POCKET MYTH opens its second guide contest

POCKET MYTH opens its second guide contest: submit a beginner guide from March 28 to April 7 and win Crystals, Shiny SSRs and limited frames.

Event Schedule

  • Submission period: March 28 to April 7
  • Judging period: April 8 to April 14
  • Results announced: April 16 (Excellence Award) and April 22 (Popularity Award)

Submission Requirements

Theme: New Player Rescue Plan (beginner-oriented guides).

Accepted content directions:

  • A 30-day growth guide for new players (resource allocation, story speedruns, solutions for getting stuck)
  • Gym challenge tips (team composition, type matchups)
  • Basic training guides (IVs, evolution materials, daily quests)
  • Recommended openings for new players (Pokémon choices, free-to-play power growth)

Please note:

  • Only beginner-oriented guides are accepted; other directions will be covered in follow-up events
  • Authors must complete one round of content editing with the official team
  • Outstanding guides will be collected on the official platform

How to Enter

  • Publish a post on Xiaohongshu with the hashtag #POCKETMythGuide# and mention @POCKETMyth
  • Long guides may be published in several parts, with the part number and the total number of parts labeled
  • Image-based guides must be clear and readable

Rewards

  • Participation Award (qualified submission): Shiny Coins x10 + Crystals x666
  • Outstanding Guide Award: one Shiny SSR of your choice + limited avatar frame + chat frame (90 days) + Crystals x888
  • Popular Guide Award: limited title Star of Outstanding Creation

Reward distribution:

  • Rewards are sent within 5 working days after the public announcement
  • The official Xiaohongshu account will contact authors by direct message

Other Notes

  • Submitting a guide is taken as authorization for official use
  • Plagiarism is strictly prohibited; violators will be disqualified

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
