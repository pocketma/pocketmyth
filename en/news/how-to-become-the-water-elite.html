How to become the Water Elite in POCKET MYTH

Full movesets, held items, Natures and EV spreads for eight Water-type picks, plus a recommended attacker, wall and two-support lineup.

1. Kyogre

  • Held item: Binding Band
  • Nature: Relaxed
  • Moves: Origin Pulse / Whirlpool / Rest / Sheer Cold
  • Tera: Grass
  • EVs: 200 HP, 200 Defense, 110 Sp. Atk

2. Dracovish (physical attacker, strong)

  • Held item: Choice Scarf
  • Ability: Strong Jaw
  • Nature: Adamant / Jolly
  • Moves: Fishious Rend / Crunch / Psychic Fangs / Rest
  • EVs: Speed and Attack

Other physical attackers in the same slot include Swampert and Cloyster (Shell Smash with White Herb).

3. Lapras (Perish Song user, holds a Dynamax Ball, special wall)

  • Held item: Binding Band
  • Nature: Calm
  • Moves: Perish Song / Whirlpool / Sheer Cold / Rest
  • EVs: HP and Sp. Def

4. Gyarados (Intimidate user, learns Dragon Tail to stop opponents stacking passives)

  • Held item: Leftovers / White Herb
  • Nature: Adamant / Jolly
  • Moves: Dragon Dance / Rest / Dragon Tail / Waterfall
  • Tera: Dragon / Flying / Grass
  • EVs: Attack and Speed

Volcanion can fill the same role (it learns Roar).

5. Quagsire (Unaware and unstoppable, physical wall)

  • Held item: Leftovers
  • Nature: Impish
  • Moves: Recover / Infestation / Toxic / Yawn
  • EVs: HP and Defense

6. Pyukumuku (Unaware and unstoppable, shiny available from events)

  • Held item: Red Card
  • Nature: depends on the team, either Sp. Def or Defense
  • Moves: Toxic / Pain Split / Taunt / Recover
  • EVs: max HP plus Sp. Def or Defense depending on the team

Other support picks include Dondozo (slows the opponent and soaks damage), Toxapex (Toxic plus recovery), Gastrodon (sets entry hazards), Milotic (Haze plus Dragon Tail), and Gorebyss and Huntail (Baton Pass).

7. Tapu Fini (hazard remover)

  • Held item: Binding Band when running Whirlpool, otherwise Leftovers
  • Nature: HP-boosting
  • Moves: Taunt (Whirlpool) / Defog / Haze / Nature's Madness
  • EVs: max HP plus Defense (or Sp. Def)

8. Manaphy (shiny from the point shop)

  • Held item: Leftovers
  • Nature: Timid
  • Moves: Tail Glow / Scald / Energy Ball / Dazzling Gleam
  • EVs: Sp. Atk and Speed

Special attackers that work the same way include Shell Smash Blastoise, Walking Wake and mixed Greninja.

Recommended composition: one physical attacker, one special attacker, one physical wall, one special wall and two supports for an attrition playstyle.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
