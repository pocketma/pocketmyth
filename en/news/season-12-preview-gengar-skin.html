POCKET MYTH Season 12 adds a Gengar skin and talents

Season 12 runs November 6 to December 3 with the Ultimate Talent tree, a new Gengar skin and updated BP rules.

Hello trainers, Joy here! The new season is about to begin, which means it is time to unlock another round of season content. Today I am bringing you the full preview of POCKET MYTH Season 12 — grab a seat and let's see what is coming.

Season 12 dates

Season 12 runs from November 6 to December 3, 2026.

Ultimate Talent is the theme

The Talent Tree is the headline system this season. Battles and rank progression earn Normal Talent Points and Advanced Talent Points, which you spend freely to build your own combat style.

Three talent trees

  • Offense — boosts damage, accuracy and combo effects
  • Defense — improves resistance, counterplay and survivability
  • Utility — raises speed, defense, HP and other general stats

Talents can be reset at any time, so you can reshape your build around a different team without starting over.

Season 12 base rules

  • Level is capped at 60 and quality at orange
  • Active: IVs, EVs, friendship, nature and ability
  • Inactive: Dynamax, Alpha, plates, badges, Stellar and awakened attributes

Pokémon restrictions

Ultra beasts are not allowed; all other generations are open. Every Pokémon must have a CP of at least 2,000, and duplicates are banned — a shiny and its normal form count as the same Pokémon.

System uses

Mega Evolution can be used 3 times and Terastallization 3 times per battle.

BP changes

BP mode opens at King tier and above. The draft is shorter than before: from six Pokémon, pick three in a 1-2-2-1 order for a faster, tighter match.

How to win

Every opposing Pokémon knocked out scores 1 point. The first trainer to 3 points wins the battle.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
