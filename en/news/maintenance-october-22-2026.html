POCKET MYTH maintenance on October 22 fixes three moves

Maintenance on October 22 from 08:00 to 10:00 fixes three ability and move interactions, with the standard compensation pack.

POCKET MYTH will go offline for maintenance on October 22, 2026 from 08:00 to 10:00, expected to take about 3 hours. If the work overruns, the servers will reopen later.

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Water Ring's healing-over-time effect not being passed on by Baton Pass
  • Fixed contact moves from a Pokémon with Unseen Fist not ignoring the opponent's Protect
  • Fixed Dragon Tail from a Pokémon with Infiltrator not forcing a target behind Substitute to switch out

Maintenance compensation

600 Crystals + 10 Gacha Tickets + 2 Shiny Coins.

Thank you for your understanding — remember to claim the pack after the update.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
