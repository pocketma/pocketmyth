Pink Rose festival brings Dark Lugia to POCKET MYTH

The Pink Rose festival runs June 27–30 with a themed sign-in, a tribute for Dark Lugia, the Eevee table, a festival shop, an album reward and limited outfits.

Trainers — the Pink Rose Flower Festival opens soon. It brings a set of casual modes, themed sign-in rewards, a limited Pokémon pool and new outfits and mounts.

1. Pink Rose daily sign-in (June 27 – June 30)

Log in each day to take part in the themed sign-in. Free rewards include Mystery Capsules, Ability Stones, Crystals, Gacha Tickets and event tribute items; the paid sign-in pass adds a large number of tribute items, with the cumulative reward being the limited Shiny Hisuian Typhlosion.

2. Festival tribute (June 27 – June 30)

Use the event's exclusive items to make offerings and raise your Heart level, unlocking tiered rewards. The final reward includes the limited Dark Lugia and 2 rare UR TM selection packs.

Every offering can also drop Dark Lugia shards, Nature Mint shard packs, Silver Crown shard packs, Magic Cube packs and UR universal held-item shards.

3. Eevee match-up (June 27 – June 29)

Pick your lucky-colour Eevee and spend Crystals on card packs to collect Eevees. Standard table: each Eevee collected settles into 1 starter shard selection pack. Advanced table: each Eevee collected settles into 1 high-grade material selection pack.

4. Festival exchange shop (June 27 – June 30)

Collect event items and trade them in the festival shop. This round adds Tera Shards and Dynamax Candy, alongside the usual ultra-beast evolution stone boxes, Dark Orb shards, ultra-beast exclusive held-item boxes, Silver Crown packs, Nature Mint packs and legendary evolution stones.

5. Battle playground (June 27 – June 30)

A spot-the-pair challenge: find the two identical Pokémon on screen to claim universal card packs and festival tribute items.

6. Pokémon album (June 27 – June 30)

The album is refreshed for the event. Complete the specified legendary sets to claim the limited Pokémon Shaymin.

7. Supreme Pokémon pool (June 27 – June 30)

A limited Supreme pool featuring Dark Rayquaza, Eternatus, Ultra Necrozma, Dark Dialga, Dark Lugia and Rising Sun Solgaleo.

8. New limited outfits and mounts (June 27 – June 28)

New looks and mounts are available for two days only: the Jiang Diandian outfit, the Silver Ash Knight look and the Yveltal exclusive mount.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
