Morning Dew Flowers closes out summer in POCKET MYTH

Morning Dew Flowers runs August 28–31 with sign-in rewards, a flower tribute for Full Moon Lunala, a flip-card mode and an album reward.

Summer break is ending and autumn is quietly arriving. POCKET MYTH's "Morning Dew Flowers" event opens soon with several modes running together.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Morning Dew Flowers · daily sign-in (August 28 – August 31)

A four-day sign-in runs during the event. Daily rewards include Morning Dew clusters, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Morning Dew Flowers · festival tribute (August 28 – August 31)

Collect Flowers and offer them as tribute to raise your Heart level. Tier rewards include Morning Dew Vouchers for the exchange shop. The grand prize is Full Moon Lunala.

Morning Dew Flowers · battle playground (August 28 – August 31)

A flip-and-match challenge; clear the stages to claim cluster items, universal card packs and other rewards at no cost.

Morning Dew Flowers · album collection (August 28 – August 31)

Complete the album to claim Shiny Lucario and its Lucarionite Mega Stone.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
