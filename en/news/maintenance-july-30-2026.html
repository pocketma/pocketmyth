POCKET MYTH maintenance on July 30 adds six Mega forms

Maintenance on July 30 adds six new ZA Mega Evolutions, with the usual compensation pack.

POCKET MYTH will go offline for maintenance on July 30, 2026 from 08:00 to 10:00, expected to take about 2 hours. If the work is not finished on time, the servers will reopen later.

New content

New ZA Mega Evolutions: Pyroar, Eternal Flower Floette, Malamar, Barbaracle, Dragalge and Drampa.

Maintenance compensation

600 Crystals + 10 Gacha Tickets + 2 Shiny Coins.

Thanks for bearing with us — log in after the update to collect the pack.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
