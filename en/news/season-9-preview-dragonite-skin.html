Season 9 brings a Dragonite skin to POCKET MYTH

Season 9 runs August 5 to August 31 with the Ultimate Talent tree, a Dragonite skin, revised BP rules and a level-60 rule set.

Hello trainers, Joy here! The new season is about to begin, and that means another round of season content to unlock.

Season 9 dates

Season 9 runs from August 5 to August 31, 2026.

Ultimate Talent is the theme

The Talent Tree system arrives this season: battles and rank progression grant Normal and Advanced Talent Points, which you spend to build a personal combat style.

Three talent trees

  • Offense — damage, accuracy and combo effects
  • Defense — resistance, counterplay and survivability
  • Utility — speed, defense, HP and other general stats

Talents can be reset, so you can adjust tactics and team composition freely.

Season 9 base rules

  • Level capped at 60, quality capped at orange
  • Active: IVs, EVs, friendship, nature, ability
  • Inactive: Dynamax, Alpha, plates, badges, Stellar, awakened attributes

Pokémon restrictions

Ultra beasts are not allowed; all other generations are open. CP must be at least 2,000, and duplicates are banned — shiny and normal forms count as one Pokémon.

System uses

Mega Evolution can be used 3 times and Terastallization 6 times per battle.

BP changes

BP mode opens at King tier and above. The draft is shortened: from six Pokémon, select three in a 1-2-2-1 order.

How to win

Each opposing Pokémon knocked out earns 1 point; the first trainer to 3 points wins.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
