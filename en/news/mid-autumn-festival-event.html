POCKET MYTH celebrates Mid-Autumn with fireworks and SP Abyss

Mid-Autumn runs September 24–28 with a five-day sign-in, a fireworks tribute for SP Abyss Sword & Shield, a dice board and the Eevee lucky-colour table.

The Mid-Autumn Festival is coming, and POCKET MYTH is celebrating with a full week of events.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Five-day sign-in (September 24 – September 28)

Daily rewards include Autumn Fireworks, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Fireworks celebration (September 24 – September 28)

Set off fireworks for a chance at server-wide red packets and festival luck, plus upgrade resources and rare items.

This round's fireworks rewards include Gold, Crystals and Mystery Capsules, with a chance at Gold and Silver Crowns, pseudo-legendary universal shards and high-grade held items. The headline prize is SP Abyss Sword & Shield.

Autumn Night vouchers (September 24 – September 28)

Fireworks have a chance to drop Autumn Night Vouchers. Trade them in the exchange shop before the event ends — unused vouchers cannot be used afterwards.

Dice board (September 24 – September 28)

The classic roll-and-move board returns with its own resource track. Rewards include a Shiny Eevee selection pack, Ancient Beast shards and Starfield Beast shards, with a chance to unlock Shiny Raging Bolt, Walking Wake, Gouging Fire and other rare Pokémon.

Pick your lucky Eevee (September 26 – September 28)

Buy card packs with Crystals and collect Eevees.

Scoring

  • Lucky colour drawn: +1
  • Three in a row: +2
  • Clear the table: +3
  • Full family photo: +6

Exchange rewards

  • Standard table: each Eevee settles into 1 starter shard selection pack
  • Advanced table: each Eevee settles into 1 high-grade material selection pack

Different ratings add extra rewards on top.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
