Autumn Bloom event brings SP Rayquaza to POCKET MYTH

Autumn Bloom runs October 11–14 with a four-day sign-in, a flower tribute for SP Rayquaza, a flip-card mode and a Caterpie evolution event.

POCKET MYTH's "Autumn Bloom" event opens soon with several modes running together.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Four-day sign-in (October 11 – October 14)

Daily rewards include Autumn Bloom clusters, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Flower tribute (October 11 – October 14)

Collect Flowers and offer them as tribute to raise your Heart level, earn tier rewards and receive Flower Vouchers for the exchange shop. The grand prize this round is SP Rayquaza.

Flip-card challenge (October 11 – October 14)

Clear the matching stages to claim Flowers, universal card packs and other rewards for free.

Caterpie evolution (October 11 – October 14)

The Caterpie evolution event opens for a limited time. Once 100 gift packs have been purchased across the server the evolution path unlocks, and the first 800 trainers to reach the goal can evolve Caterpie into Rayquaza.

Album collection (October 11 – October 14)

Complete the album to claim Shiny Garchomp and its evolution stone.

Starlight Treasure (October 11 – October 14)

Choose your own rewards from a customisable pool. All prizes are guaranteed within 12 rounds, with a chance of bonus rounds.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
