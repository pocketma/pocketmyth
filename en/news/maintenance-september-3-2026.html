POCKET MYTH maintenance on September 3 adds new skins

Maintenance on September 3 adds character skins and a Miraidon title, swaps the World Championship scene and fixes three battle issues.

POCKET MYTH will go offline for maintenance on September 3, 2026 from 08:00 to 10:00, expected to take about 2 hours. If the work runs long, the servers will reopen later.

New content

  • Several new character skins
  • New title: National #1 Miraidon

Optimisations

  • The World Championship battle scene is replaced with the Champion battle scene

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an incorrect damage value in Photon Geyser's calculation
  • Fixed Quick Claw's prompt timing so that a trigger in the current turn takes effect in the current turn
  • Fixed Pokémon knocked out by weather damage in Champion's Road sometimes not scoring

Maintenance compensation

600 Crystals + 10 Gacha Tickets + 2 Shiny Coins.

Thank you for your patience — remember to claim the pack after the update.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
