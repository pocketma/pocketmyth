Splendid Flowers weekend brings Primal Groudon to POCKET MYTH

Splendid Flowers runs July 30–August 2 with a four-day sign-in, a flower tribute for Primal Groudon, an album reward and a flip-card challenge.

Warm wind, fields of flowers and a long, bright road ahead. POCKET MYTH's "Splendid Flowers" event opens soon with several modes running together.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Splendid Flowers · daily sign-in (July 30 – August 2)

A four-day sign-in runs during the event. Daily rewards include Splendid clusters, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Splendid Flowers · festival tribute (July 30 – August 2)

The fan-favourite flower tribute returns. Collect Flowers and offer them to raise your Heart level; tier rewards include Flower Vouchers for the exchange shop. The grand prize is Primal Land Lord Groudon.

Splendid Flowers · festival album (July 30 – August 2)

Complete the album to claim Shiny Slaking and its outfit.

Splendid Flowers · festival challenge (July 30 – August 2)

A flip-and-match challenge; clear stages to earn Splendid clusters and universal card packs, and keep collecting Flowers for the tribute rewards.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
