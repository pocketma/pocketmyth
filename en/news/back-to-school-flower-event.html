POCKET MYTH sends players back to school with Dark Ho-Oh

Back-to-school runs September 4-7 with a four-day sign-in, a flower tribute for Dark Ho-Oh, flip-card mode, album rewards and Starlight Treasure.

POCKET MYTH's "Flower Reception" event opens soon with several modes running together.

Note: the images are for reference only. Exact times and rewards follow the in-game schedule.

Flower Reception · daily sign-in (September 4 – September 7)

A four-day sign-in runs during the event. Daily rewards include Flowers, items, Ability Stones and Gacha Tickets.

Flower Reception · festival tribute (September 4 – September 7)

Collect Flowers and offer them as tribute to raise your Heart level. Tier rewards include Flower Vouchers for the exchange shop. The grand prize is Dark Ho-Oh.

Flower Reception · battle playground (September 4 – September 7)

A flip-and-match challenge; clear the stages to claim Flowers, universal card packs and other rewards at no cost.

Flower Reception · album collection (September 4 – September 7)

Complete the album to claim a Shiny ultra-rare Pokémon selection pack.

Flower Reception · Starlight Treasure (September 4 – September 7)

Open the exclusive Starlight Treasure and choose your own pool. All prizes are guaranteed within 12 rounds, with bonus-round chances.

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
