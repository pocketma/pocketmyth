POCKET MYTH adds Jirachi and Expedition Road on January 8

The January 8 update adds UR Jirachi, two new skins and the Expedition Road mode, retires Trial Grounds and fixes three battle issues.

The January 8, 2026 update brings new Pokémon, new skins and a new mode.

New content

  • New UR Pokémon Miracle Star Jirachi
  • New Dark Lugia skin: Starlight Wings
  • New Gengar skin: Party Mood
  • New mode Expedition Road, opening after the update; Trial Grounds closes permanently and Expedition Road uses exactly the same reward table

Optimisations

  • Champion's Road Season 2 base round time adjusted to 40 seconds

Bug fixes

  • Fixed Endure mistakenly triggering a fainting effect
  • Fixed targeting for Dark Void in double battles
  • Fixed Champion's Road double battles becoming unresponsive in some situations

Event times and rewards follow the in-game schedule. POCKET MYTH is a live game, so details may be adjusted by the development team.
